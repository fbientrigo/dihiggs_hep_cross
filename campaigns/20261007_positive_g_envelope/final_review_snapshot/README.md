# Independent review evidence

These files and the small probe runner provide supplementary independent generic-basis evidence, separate from the campaign's own output tables. The initial bulk review artifacts were supplied by the read-only reviewer; `generic_probe.cpp` was supplied at `/tmp/preflight_review_probe.cpp` and is now included with a reproducible runner.

- `all125_independent_recheck.csv`: latest frozen physical-route adapter reevaluation of the current 122 search-best rows plus 3 Y2=0 anchors; all 125 passed, with 26 precision warnings and maximum reported signed-coupling residual `1.85e-7 GeV`. This is not the generic-THDM route.
- `generic_refined_freshTHDM.csv` and `stable_HB_refined.json`: fresh generic-THDM probe on five high-t candidates, plus stable Higgs-basis comparisons. Reported maximum Higgs-basis mass residual is below `1.54e-7 GeV`, with signed-coupling residual below `5e-14 GeV` for the high-t set. Earlier generic review also covered 54 checkpoint candidates in archived review material.
- `fine_perturbations.csv`: 40 independent high-t coordinate perturbations; all 20 lambda1 perturbations pass and all 20 Delta_g perturbations remain numerically unresolved. This leaves the local high-t frontier open.
- `generic_probe.cpp`, `run_generic_probe.sh`, and `run_generic_probe.py`: source and compile/input/output steps for an independent generic-basis recheck of the three inward representatives. The runner compiles a fresh `THDM.cpp`; its translated signed couplings agree with the campaign values, including the `sba=-1` field-sign cases.
- `reviewer_latest_low_t_generic_raw.csv`: latest reviewer generic-basis probe output at the transferred shared node `tanβ=0.5` for all three masses. It is headerless and follows the output-column order documented in `generic_probe.cpp`; all three pass scalar controls, and `sba × g_generic` gives the positive signed coupling in the appendix convention.

The broad 125-point review result and high-t perturbation CSVs are reviewer-produced evidence; their original bulk-run transcript is not bundled. The representative generic probe can be rerun from the workspace with `./independent_review/run_generic_probe.sh`. It uses only generic spectrum/coupling and scalar controls, no decay tools. The campaign's canonical scalar-only adapter and full reproduction command are at the package root.
