A 0.1 absolute (2.5e-9 relative) change in tan(beta) moves m_phi by
~7 GeV, and the response is not monotonic.  At tan(beta)=4e7 the
generic-basis spectrum is not a numerically stable function of its
inputs, so m_phi must be an INPUT (physical-basis route), never an
output of set_param_gen.
